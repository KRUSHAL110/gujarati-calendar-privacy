Privacy Policy

App: Gujarati Calendar 2027 – Panchang
Last updated: 2026

This app is an offline Hindu calendar and panchang. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum.

Information we handle

  • Location: If you tap “Detect my location”, we read your approximate GPS location once to compute local sunrise/sunset and panchang. It is used on-device only and is never uploaded or shared. You can instead pick a city manually.
  • Personal tithis & settings: Your saved tithis (birthdays, anniversaries, shraddha) and preferences are stored only on your device.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Ads

We show ads via Google AdMob to keep the app free. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve ads. In the EU/UK we ask for your consent (Google UMP) before serving personalised ads. You can remove all ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

In-app purchases

The “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details.

Notifications

With your permission, we schedule local notifications on your device to remind you of festivals and your saved tithis. No notification data leaves your device.

Children

This app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
